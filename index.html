About CraftCalc

CraftCalc is a free, independent fan-made Minecraft utility. It helps players calculate crafting materials and explore recipes.

The calculator runs directly in your browser. No account, subscription, or external API is required.

CraftCalc is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Mojang Studios or Microsoft. Minecraft is a trademark of Microsoft.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is CraftCalc free?

Yes. The calculator is designed to be completely free to use.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The website is designed to work on phones, tablets, and computers.

Does it require an account?

No account is required.

Does it work for Java and Bedrock?

The calculator focuses on recipes shared between the editions. Edition-specific recipes can be added later.
